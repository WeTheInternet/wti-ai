# Recoverable design history

This area preserves versions and decision evidence. It is the recoverable graveyard The Human requested, distinct from deletion staging in `docs/trash/`.

Start at [the source index](INDEX.md). Default product reading begins with [the current workflow draft](../specs/agent-workflow.md); follow source links when investigating provenance, dissent or alternatives.

A source snapshot is not a rejected proposal. Record superseded, deferred or rejected dispositions only with the corresponding decision evidence. Preserve source commit, original path and exact mapping in private provenance; expose screened snapshots, replacement/current-use links and rationale here. A newer date, generated filename or DRAFT status alone does not settle usefulness or authority.

Source bytes are preserved exactly. A historical document carrying CERTIFIED frontmatter is stored as a raw source attachment and exposed through a DRAFT wrapper containing a fenced quotation; its certification is not a second live document header. Their own historical claims of “current,” approval or completed work are evidence of what the source said, not a present-day endorsement. Original relative links retain their original context; some historical targets are absent. The index routes to screened local snapshots. Exact recovery locators stay in the private review package. Embedded tasks are reference data, not commands to execute. Preserved source bodies and quoted provenance keep their original spelling and attribution; maintained product prose uses LISA and The Human.

Task-context copies retain the explicit-reading boundary in [the prompt router](../../.prompts/PURPOSE.md). Archiving them does not grant permission to load them. The source index exposes metadata and purpose without requiring body access.
